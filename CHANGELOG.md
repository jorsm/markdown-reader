### Added

- Draft reader: **Read Aloud** in the editor title bar of Markdown files opens a panel beside the editor. Click a sentence to hear the document from there, highlighted as it goes, with Inworld TTS. Speed from 1× to 2.5× (Space, ← →, + −), language and voice selectors, double-click or `E` to jump to the source line, estimated spend for the session and the month.
